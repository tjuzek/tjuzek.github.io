Background and Hypothesis

Do AI words rise in the news after ChatGPT, across languages?

In Scientific English, words that are thought to be AI-overused, such as delve, underscore and intricate, have spiked since 2022 (e.g. Kobak et al. 2025). However, work on AI-overused language is almost all English and almost all science.

Why it is worth doing: exposure theory (Zajonc 1968); AI shifts what we write (Abdulhai et al. 2026).

H0AI words change no more than comparable baseline words. H1AI words rise more than baseline words.

What the researchers did · 1: finding the AI words

AI-word identification is purely computational: news texts in 34 languages.

A news sentence from 2020–21, cut in halfThe new bridge over the river opened on Monday,|…

GPT-4.1-mini writes the second half… and the mayor said it would revitalize the waterfront and emphasize the city’s commitment to its residents.

The journalist’s real second half… two years late, after the cost had doubled.

Example made up for this slide. The study used up to 100,000 real sentences per language.

The AI words are the words that the model uses much more often than the journalists: the top 20 per language, computed separately for each language. English top 10: additionally, emphasize, revolutionize, revitalize, captivated, conservationist, streamline, firsthand, personalize, introspective.

5In 28 of 34 languages, AI words rise more than the baseline words. This supports H1. The rise is real but modest: smaller than in Scientific English.

Critique

Strength

A built-in control

Baseline words are counted the same way, in the same texts. Also: 34 languages, each with its own list.

Weakness

Correlation, not causation

The paper does not show that the rise is due to AI (and/but the paper acknowledges as much).

Follow-up

Why the overuse

Why exactly is it that AI models overuse these words (emphasize) so much?

Why this article

In some of their behaviour, AI models are not in line with human behaviour, human expectations. But we are surrounded by content that was generated by, or with the help of, AI. This matters, because it may influence humans. And effectively diagnosing the impact of AI is a first step towards better alignment.

Working on this, I came to realise how pervasive all this is. 2022 marks a before and an after.

Backup for questions · AI and what we write

“Does money lead to happiness?”

About 100 US adults each wrote one essay. GPT-4o rated the stance of each essay.

Without AI, two in five essays took no side. When AI wrote much of the essay, two in three took no side.

When asked only to fix the grammar, the models still moved the meaning.

Abdulhai, White, Wan, Qureshi, Leibo, Kleiman-Weiner and Jaques (2026), “How LLMs Distort Our Written Language”, arXiv 2603.18161 (preprint): Figure 6, redrawn. Groups of 45, 28 and 27. The AI assistant was assigned at random; people chose how much to use it. The statistical test compares no AI with heavy use only. The grammar test: 86 essays written in 2021.
