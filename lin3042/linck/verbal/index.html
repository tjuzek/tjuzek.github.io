Main task · language production  about 3 minutes

Its role in the study

Main task: language production. The other task that tests the hypothesis: how easily do words come, in English and in Spanish? If immersion holds English back, immersed learners should come up with fewer English words. They did, and with more Spanish ones (Figure 3).

How it works

You get a category, say animals, and 30 seconds. Think of as many as you can, in your head, and tap +1 for each one (on a laptop, the space bar). If you prefer, write them on paper and tap as you write.

Do you have a second language, even a bit? Spanish from school counts.
When is your birthday? This only sets which language comes first.
