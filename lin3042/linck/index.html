Linck et al. (2009): the four tasks

Toy versions of the four tasks in Linck, Kroll and Sunderman (2009), a couple of minutes each. Please use one device, and do each task once.

AI disclaimer. Content guided by Tommie Juzek; built upon his prompting by an AI assistant (Claude Opus 5.5, Anthropic); reviewed and approved by Tommie Juzek.

The study

Linck, Kroll and Sunderman (2009) compared two groups of American students learning Spanish: 25 on a semester abroad in Spain, and 20 in the classroom at home. Their hypothesis: immersion helps learners because it holds back (inhibits) their native language, English.

What the four tasks are for

  • Main task Two main tasks test the hypothesis. They measure how easily the learners get at their English while Spanish is in play: translation recognition (comprehension) and verbal fluency (production).
  • Background check Two background checks measure general cognitive ability, not language: reading span (working memory) and the Simon task (inhibitory control). They show whether the two groups started out alike, so that differences in the main tasks can be put down to immersion, and not to one group being better at this kind of thing anyway.

In each task, a coloured band after every answer tells you what kind of trial it was; the practice round explains each kind.

Main task · language comprehension

1 Translation recognition

about 3 minutes

Is the English word the translation of the Spanish one? Starts with a one-minute word list. In the paper: immersed learners were hardly slowed by English look-alikes of the Spanish word (cara – CARD); classroom learners were.

Main task · language production

2 Verbal fluency

about 3 minutes

As many animals (and so on) as you can think of in 30 seconds. In the paper: immersed learners came up with more Spanish words, but fewer English ones.

Background check · cognitive ability (working memory)

3 Reading span

about 3 minutes

Judge sentences while you hold on to a growing list of words. In the paper: the two groups were alike (51.5 and 51.8 of 80 words).

Start

Background check · cognitive ability (inhibitory control)

4 Simon task

about 2 minutes

Answer by a square’s colour, and ignore where it is. In the paper: the groups were not quite alike (38 against 20 ms); the classroom group did better.
