LIN 3042 Applied Linguistics · Fall 2026

Linck et al. (2009): the four tasks

Toy versions of the tasks in today’s article, a couple of minutes each. Please use one device, and do each task once.

AI disclaimer. Content guided by Tommie Juzek; built upon his prompting by an AI assistant (Claude Opus 5.5, Anthropic); reviewed and approved by Tommie Juzek.

Linck, Kroll and Sunderman (2009) compared two groups of American students learning Spanish: 25 on a semester abroad in Spain, and 20 in the classroom at home. Everyone did the same four tasks. Here you can try toy versions. After each answer, a coloured band tells you what kind of trial it was; the practice rounds explain each kind.

1 Translation recognition

linguistic · comprehension · about 3 min

Is the English word the translation of the Spanish one? Starts with a one-minute word list.

In the paper: do English words that look like the Spanish word still get in the way?

2 Verbal fluency

linguistic · production · about 3 min

As many animals (and so on) as you can think of in 30 seconds.

In the paper: did immersion make English words come more slowly?

3 Reading span

cognitive · working memory · about 3 min

Judge sentences while you hold on to a growing list of words.

In the paper: a check that the two groups started out alike.

4 Simon task

cognitive · inhibitory control · about 2 min

Answer by a square’s colour, and ignore where it is.

In the paper: another check that the two groups started out alike.

These are toy versions: the words, timings and numbers of trials are ours, not the paper’s. Your answers stay in your browser; nothing is stored or sent.

1 · Translation recognition

First, one minute. Learn these Spanish words as well as you can. If you know Spanish already, you have a head start, just like the learners in the paper.

Then the task. A Spanish word appears, then an English word in CAPITALS. Is the English word its translation? As fast as you can:

  • the translation: tap Yes (on a laptop, press J);
  • not the translation: tap No (on a laptop, press F).

Six practice trials first, each with an explanation, then 24 for real (about a minute and a half).

2 · Verbal fluency

You get a category, say animals, and 30 seconds. Think of as many as you can, in your head, and tap +1 for each one (on a laptop, the space bar). If you prefer, write them on paper and tap as you write.

Do you have a second language, even a bit? Spanish from school counts.
When is your birthday? This only sets which language comes first.

3 · Reading span

You see a sentence. Does it make sense? Tap Makes sense (J) or Doesn’t (F). Then a word appears for one second: remember it. After a few sentences, type all the words of that set, in any order.

The sets get longer: 2, 3, 4, then 5 words. One practice set of 2 first, with explanations.

4 · Simon task

A coloured square appears to the left or the right of a +. Answer by its colour only:

  • a blue square: tap Blue, the left button (on a laptop, F);
  • a yellow square: tap Yellow, the right button (J).

Where the square appears does not matter. As fast as you can, with as few mistakes as you can.

Six practice trials first, each with an explanation, then 48 for real (about a minute and a half).
