Its role in the study

Background check: cognitive ability (working memory). Not a test of the hypothesis, and not about Spanish. It measures how much you can hold in mind while doing something else. Linck et al. used it to check that the two groups started out alike. They did (51.5 and 51.8 of 80 words, Table 1), so a difference in the main tasks cannot be put down to working memory.

How it works

You see a sentence. Does it make sense? Tap Makes sense (J) or Doesn’t (F). Then a word appears for one second: remember it. After a few sentences, type all the words of that set, in any order.

The sets get longer: 2, 3, 4, then 5 words. One practice set of 2 first, with explanations.
